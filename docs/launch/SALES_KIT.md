# Vroom Sales Kit

## Offer

- Product: vroom.today, online booking and operating cabinet for driving schools.
- Price: 4 990 RUB per month for one school account during the manual launch stage.
- Payment: manual bank/card transfer. Access is extended after operator confirmation.
- Best first customer: owner or director of a small or medium driving school with 5-40 instructors.

## What Problem We Sell

- Students write in messengers and call administrators to find a driving slot.
- Instructors have empty windows that are hard to see in time.
- Directors do not see debts, missing documents, idle students, and near-term workload in one place.
- Excel and chats work for a small volume, then become a source of mistakes.

## Demo Script

1. Show the public school page and book a lesson as a student.
2. Open the student cabinet: next lesson, instructor, school contacts, status.
3. Open the admin Today screen: what needs attention today.
4. Open Schedule: free and booked windows, cancellation/reschedule logic.
5. Open Students: status, debt, missing documents, next lesson, CSV import/export.
6. Open Settings: launch checklist, booking limits, debt/document restrictions.
7. Close with the 3-day pilot plan and manual support.

## Objections

- “У нас уже Excel.” Answer: Excel остается источником для импорта, но ежедневная запись и свободные окна становятся видны ученику и администратору.
- “Админы привыкли к WhatsApp.” Answer: WhatsApp остается каналом общения, vroom убирает ручной подбор времени и потерянные записи.
- “Нужны оплаты внутри.” Answer: на первом этапе оплаты подтверждаются вручную; продукт уже показывает долги и статусы.
- “Страшно переносить данные.” Answer: начинаем с пилота: 1 филиал, 5-10 инструкторов, часть учеников, тестовая неделя.

## Honest Scope

- Ready: public booking, student cabinet, admin schedule, students, payments/debts, documents, settings, lead capture.
- Manual: payments, onboarding, legal finalization per customer, pilot support.
- In progress after pilots: stronger server-backed auth, password recovery, customer-specific integrations.
