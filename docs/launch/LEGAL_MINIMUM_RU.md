# Legal Minimum RU

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Before broad production use with real personal data, final documents should be reviewed by a Russian personal-data lawyer.

## Required Before Real Customers

- Public terms of service and privacy policy are available at `/terms` and `/privacy`.
- Student registration includes consent to terms, privacy policy, and personal-data processing.
- Lead form includes consent text and links.
- Customer school understands that it must have a lawful basis and student consents before uploading real student data.
- Support can export, correct, or delete student records on request from the school.

## Data Categories

- FIO, phone, email.
- Driving category, branch, instructor, group.
- Bookings, cancellations, reschedules, progress and confirmed hours.
- Documents and document statuses.
- Manual payment statuses, debt, remaining amount.

## Risk Notes

- Avoid promising full 152-FZ compliance until documents, hosting, subprocessors, and customer contracts are reviewed.
- Do not collect unnecessary sensitive data in early pilots.
- Limit employee access by role and school.
